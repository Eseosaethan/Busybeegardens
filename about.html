About us

BusyBee Gardening Services is a female-run business dedicated to transforming and maintaining beautiful outdoor spaces. With over two decades of experience, we pride ourselves on delivering high-quality, personalized gardening solutions for homes and businesses.

Our services include:

  1. Garden Clearance & Seasonal Cleanup – Spring and Autumn tidy-ups to keep your garden fresh and healthy
  2. Complete Garden Re-Design – Using advanced 3D virtual tour software for a realistic preview of your dream garden
  3. Regular Maintenance – Weekly or monthly plans tailored to your needs
  4. Grass cutting Professional lawn care for a neat and polished look
  5. Patio & Fencing – Stylish and durable outdoor enhancements
  6. Tree and shrub cutting – Expert pruning for safety and aesthetics
  7. Water Features & Ponds – Adding tranquility and elegance to your garden
